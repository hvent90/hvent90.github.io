Book Cover Size Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Data Collection

Book Cover Size Calculator does not collect personal data, does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not send page counts, trim sizes, paper selections, or calculated cover dimensions to a server.

User Input

Values entered in the app are used on device to calculate paperback cover dimensions. The app does not upload files or store user input in a backend service.

Tracking

The app does not track users across apps or websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to me@hventura.com.
