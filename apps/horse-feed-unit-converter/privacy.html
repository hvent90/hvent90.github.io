Horse Feed Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Data Collection

Horse Feed Unit Converter does not collect personal data, does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not send feed measurements to a server.

User Input

Measurements and density values entered in the app are used on device to calculate unit conversions. They are not stored in a backend service.

Tracking

The app does not track users across apps or websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to me@hventura.com.
