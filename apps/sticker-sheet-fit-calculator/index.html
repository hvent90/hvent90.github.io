Sticker Sheet Fit Calculator Support

Sticker Sheet Fit Calculator is an offline iPhone utility for estimating same-size sticker sheet layouts from sticker size, quantity, bleed, gap, margin, and rotation settings.

Contact

For support, bug reports, or App Store questions, email me@hventura.com.

Privacy

Read the app privacy policy: Sticker Sheet Fit Calculator Privacy Policy.

Scope

The app is a planning calculator. It does not create artwork, control cutting machines, or guarantee printer accuracy. Verify final production files in your own print and cut workflow.
