Candle Wax Batch Calculator Support

Candle Wax Batch Calculator is an offline iPhone utility for planning small container candle batches from jar size, fill level, wax density, fragrance load, and optional material costs.

Contact

For support, bug reports, or App Store questions, email me@hventura.com.

Privacy

Read the app privacy policy: Candle Wax Batch Calculator Privacy Policy.

Scope

The app is a planning calculator. It does not replace wax supplier data sheets, burn checks, product safety work, or candle labeling requirements.
