Candle Wax Batch Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Data Collection

Candle Wax Batch Calculator does not collect personal data, does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not send jar sizes, candle counts, wax settings, fragrance loads, cost inputs, or calculation results to a server.

User Input

Values entered in the app are used on device to calculate wax weight, fragrance oil weight, total batch weight, spare wax, and rough material cost. The app does not upload recipes or store user input in a backend service.

Tracking

The app does not track users across apps or websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to me@hventura.com.
